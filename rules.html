How to play

The Rules of Ultimate Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe

Ultimate Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe is a nested version of Tic-Tac-Toe. You are not just playing on one board: each move sends the next player to a smaller board inside the larger one, and the goal is to win the big board by winning smaller boards along the way.

1. Start with the big picture

The game contains boards inside boards inside boards. A move is made on the smallest visible grid, but every choice matters at multiple levels.

  • Each small board works like regular Tic-Tac-Toe.
  • Winning a small board claims it for your mark.
  • Three claimed boards in a line win the next larger board.

2. Where your move sends the next player

Each board works almost like regular Tic-Tac-Toe. The twist: where you choose to play controls your opponent's next move. If you imagine the middle-size board expanded to the size of the big board, the square you played in becomes the board your opponent must go to next.

  • Play in the board that has been directed for you.
  • Your square maps directly to the next board on the larger grid.
  • If that board is already finished, the next player gets a free choice instead.

3. How to win a board

A board is won the same way as classic Tic-Tac-Toe: get three of your marks in a row, column, or diagonal.

  • Rows, columns, and diagonals all count.
  • Once a board is won, it stays claimed.
  • Claimed boards help you build a line on the larger board above them.

4. Winning the whole game

The top-level board is won by claiming three smaller boards in a row, column, or diagonal. That means you are always trying to win both the current board and the larger structure above it.

If a board is already finished, treat it as locked and focus on the remaining playable spaces.
